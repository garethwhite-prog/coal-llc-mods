# End-Game Staffs & Poison Guns Arsenal

**Namespace:** $namespace  
**Compatibility:** Coal LLC v1.4.1.1+ (ModLoader 7.0.1)

---

## Overview
Injects previously unreferenced Tiers 12-15 Magic Staffs (Spinel, Uranium, Moonstone, Onyx) and all 15 Caustic Poison Guns into the equipment shop.

---

## Technical Details (For Modders)
Dynamically appends staff and poison gun .tres resources into item_unlocks.all_equipment_items and item_unlocks.all_items during mod initialization.

---

## Installation
1. Ensure **Coal LLC** has Godot ModLoader 7.0.1 installed.
2. Place the packaged .zip file into your Coal LLC/mods/ directory.
3. Manage or toggle this mod directly via the in-game **Mod Manager**.
